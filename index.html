Every player carries one bag. Small paintings eat about half a bag; jewelry and small artifacts take much less room. Some floors need at least two players to access.

3Scoped Secondary Loot

Every scoutable item is listed below, grouped by the floor you'll find it on. Fill in the value for whatever you actually photographed and leave the rest blank. Mark up to three (fewer is fine) as the Buyer's Choice — these only affect bag packing once the Elite Challenge (Step 4) is turned on. With Elite off, the ledger just optimizes for maximum bag value regardless of these picks. The Delivery Truck Crate's true value can't be known until you actually take it, so it's a checkbox — check it if you grabbed it, and it locks in at the community-estimated value. The Crisp Gallery gemstone comes in a different type every run — pick the one you scoped from its dropdown and the Heist Guide will name it. That pick is labelling only; it doesn't change any of the math.

4Elite Challenge
No
Yes

If yes, the ledger locks in your Buyer's Choice items first — the Buyer's Request and Elite Challenge bonuses are only in play if at least 2 items are marked (a single item can never satisfy the challenge) and every marked item actually fits (and is reachable by your crew size). If no, Buyer's Choice picks are ignored for packing and the ledger optimizes purely for maximum bag value.

5Crew Names (Optional)

Label each player's card on the Heist Guide page. Leave any of these blank to just show "Player 1", "Player 2", etc. Player 1 is always the host. Every other player automatically earns the Helper bonus ($100,000 Normal / $200,000 Hard) on top of their secondary loot share — this isn't something you toggle here.
